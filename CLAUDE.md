# youretfplan.com

- The site is one self-contained file: `public/index.html`. Edit it in place.
- All visible text lives in the `T` object in the script, with `en` and `it` entries. Every change to copy must be made in both languages.
- Language is picked from saved choice, then Italian time zone or browser language, else English.
- Donate links point to https://buymeacoffee.com/youretfplan.
- Pushing to `main` deploys to production through Cloudflare Workers Builds. Check the page in a headless browser (phone width, both languages, light and dark) before pushing.
- Owner: Marco. He also keeps a copy of the page as a claude.ai artifact; keep it in sync when asked.
